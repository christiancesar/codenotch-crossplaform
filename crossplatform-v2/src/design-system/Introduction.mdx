# Codenotch design system

The notch is pure black, welded to the screen edge so it reads as part of the bezel. Geometry
and motion come from the official app's Figma frame (`docs/design/frame-124-hover-tooltip.png`);
colours from the Tailwind palette on shadcn's neutral theme; type is Raleway for headings and
Space Grotesk for everything else.

- **Colors**: the shadcn theme plus the Codenotch roles (usage bands, session states, tracks).
- **Typography**: the two families and the role sizes; numbers that change are tabular.
- **Geometry**: frame measurements at scale 1.
- **Motion**: durations and the stepped indicators.

The full rules live in `crossplatform-v2/docs/design-system.md`. Use the toolbar's theme switch
to see light and dark; the notch itself is always dark.
