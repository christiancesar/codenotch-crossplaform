# Motion

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--dur-card-in` | 180 ms | Hover card in |
| `--dur-card-grace` | 250 ms | Pointer out to card out |
| `--dur-collapse` | 180 ms | Pill to tab and back |
| `--dur-cell-fade` | 120 ms | Cells fading on collapse |

Continuous indicators are **stepped** (about 10 repaints a second): a 60 fps transform in a
transparent always-on-top window stalls the desktop compositor. With reduced motion they stop.
